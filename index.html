How FoodBridge Works

From surplus inventory to community plates in four seamless, transparent steps.

01

Donor Lists Surplus

Restaurants, hotels, or caterers specify quantity, pickup deadline, allergens, and storage type.

02

NGO Claims Needed Qty

Verified charities browse local listings and request exact quantities for their daily meal distribution.

03

Volunteer Dispatched

A community volunteer accepts the route, picks up the sealed food, and transports it swiftly.

04

Nourished Community

Donation is marked complete with full transparency, zero food waste, and verified impact logging.

Why FoodBridge Stands Out

Food safety, vetted organizations, and automated distribution ensure high efficiency with zero risk.

Strict Food Safety Standards

Preparation times, pickup deadlines, allergen warnings, and thermal storage instructions are validated on every listing.

Verified Non-Profit Partners

Every charity undergoes administrative documentation verification before gaining permission to request donations.

Real-Time Volunteer Routing

Integrated logistics assign verified volunteer couriers automatically once donors approve requests.

Anti-Overselling Inventory

Portion reservations prevent double allocations, ensuring organizations get exactly what was agreed upon.

Verifiable Impact Reporting

Transparent live dashboards measure meals saved, emissions diverted, and meals served for community reports.

Lightweight & Fast

Engineered for extreme performance and ease of use, with seamless fallback offline and demo capability.
