About FoodBridge

"Turning Surplus Food into Someone's Next Meal."

We are on a mission to end food insecurity and eliminate avoidable landfill food waste by orchestrating real-time logistics between commercial kitchens, verified charities, and community volunteers.

The Reality & The Solution

Nearly 30% of Good Food is Wasted While Families Go Hungry.

Food waste isn't a supply problem—it's a logistics and timing challenge. Caterers, hotels, and restaurants frequently produce high-quality, delicious surplus meals that cannot be stored overnight. Meanwhile, local shelters and soup kitchens struggle with grocery budgets.

FoodBridge solves this bottleneck. Through automated inventory matching, verified non-profit accounts, and volunteer dispatch, surplus food is claimed and transported within hours of preparation.

Food Safety & Integrity

Our Uncompromising Safety Commitment

FoodBridge prioritizes safety at every stage. We do not claim food is safe merely because it is listed—we mandate clear disclosures, thermal tracking, and rigorous verification.

Thermal Chain Control

All cooked and dairy listings mandate storage declarations (Room Temp, Refrigerated, or Frozen). Volunteers are equipped with insulated transport bags to preserve safe temperatures.

Allergen Transparency

Donors must declare any common allergens (Gluten, Dairy, Peanuts, Tree Nuts, Eggs, Shellfish) so recipient organizations can distribute appropriately to vulnerable individuals.

Donor Legal Safety Declaration

Every listing requires a signed digital declaration that the food has been handled under certified commercial hygiene conditions and remains within safe consumption windows.
