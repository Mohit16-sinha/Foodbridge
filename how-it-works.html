End-to-End Workflow

How FoodBridge Works

A high-efficiency coordination pipeline connecting donors, charities, and volunteer couriers within a safe, accountable ecosystem.

Step-by-Step Architecture

The 4-Stage Redistribution Pipeline

Stage 01

Surplus Logging & Safety Declaration

When a commercial kitchen or caterer identifies surplus meal portions, they log into the Donor Portal and publish an advisory:

  • Food name, quantity, category & photos
  • Exact preparation time & strict pickup deadline
  • Storage temperature requirement (Refrigerated/Ambient/Frozen)
  • Allergen disclosures & signed legal safety declaration
Sample Donor Notification:
New Listing Published: 50 portions of "Warm Vegetarian Curry & Rice" published with pickup deadline at 6:00 PM.
Anti-Overselling Guard:
Portion Reservation: When Hope Feeding Mission requests 30 packets, available quantity atomically adjusts from 50 to 20.
Stage 02

Verified NGO Discovery & Portioned Claim

Approved NGOs receive in-app notifications of nearby food matching their distribution capacity. Unapproved organizations are blocked from claiming food.

  • Browse available meals on live OpenStreetMap
  • Specify custom requested portions (e.g. 30 out of 50)
  • Direct message to donor regarding service schedule
Stage 03

Donor Approval & Volunteer Courier Dispatch

The donor reviews the NGO request and confirms with a single click. The system immediately creates a dispatch mission and broadcasts it to available volunteers.

  • Volunteer accepts mission (prevents conflicting claims)
  • Turn-by-turn route: Donor Kitchen → Recipient NGO
  • Live status stepper: Accepted → Picked Up → Delivered

Stage 04

Delivery Completion & Transparent Analytics

Once the volunteer marks the mission delivered at the charity's entrance, all statistics and audit logs update instantaneously.

  • Donor receives delivery receipt and tax deduction data
  • Volunteer delivery tally increments
  • NGO records completed distribution without overhead
